ParamTatva operates on a dual-license structure. The Sanskriti Toolchain and SanOS are free for open-source development, but require a commercial license for closed-source, proprietary deployments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use the Community tier for a commercial startup?

Yes, absolutely. However, because we use the AGPL-3.0 license, if you modify or use our software to serve users over a network (like a SaaS web app), you are legally required to open-source your entire application's source code. If you want to keep your application's source code secret, you must purchase the Enterprise License.

Why AGPL-3.0 instead of MIT or Apache?

ParamTatva represents years of deep R&D into Zero-Mass computing and Sanskrit-native compilation. The AGPL ensures that if a massive corporation uses our tools to build a multi-million dollar service, they must either contribute their improvements back to the community, or pay for a commercial license to fund our continued development.

Does the Enterprise license require royalties?

No. The Enterprise license is typically structured as an annual subscription per developer seat, or a flat buyout depending on your organization's scale. There are no per-user or per-transaction royalties.
